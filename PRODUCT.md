# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

People preparing for or starting an outdoor workout who want to plan and track a walk, jog, run, or cycling activity. They choose an activity, target distance, and route preferences.

## Product Purpose

Karipa brings route planning and activity tracking together so users can find a route that fits their activity and distance, follow it, and track their workout without confusion.

## Positioning

Karipa combines route planning and activity tracking across walking, jogging, running, and cycling. Users can set a target distance and route preferences, and routes are verified by Valhalla. No comparative or exclusive market claim has been established.

## Operating Context

Users plan routes before or as they start outdoor workouts, then follow and track the activity with the app open in the foreground.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Supports walking, jogging, running, and cycling.
- Route results are verified by Valhalla.
- Activity recording must remain in the foreground.
- Offline synchronization is not supported or planned.
- Preserve existing Flutter project patterns and Android conventions.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Karipa.

## Evidence on Hand

- The existing application and its Valhalla-verified routing provide product evidence.
- No customer testimonials or quantified outcome claims were supplied; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Give all four supported activity types equal product consideration.
- Let activity, target distance, and route preferences guide route planning.
- Ground route confidence in Valhalla verification.
- Make the foreground-only tracking constraint clear; do not imply offline tracking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow standard Android accessibility practices, including readable contrast, scalable text, and accessible control labels. No additional product-specific requirements were established.
